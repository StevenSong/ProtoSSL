HEEDB CASE SELECTOR - HOW TO USE
================================

This folder lets you browse ECGs from the HEEDB test set and pick cases for
the user study. Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded
and no internet connection is needed. The data is covered by the HEEDB DUA,
so keep the folder on the encrypted drive and do not share it.

GETTING STARTED
  1. Keep this folder together. index.html, index.js and the "pdf" folder
     must stay side by side.
  2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser. Google Chrome
     or Microsoft Edge work best. If the ECG area stays blank in Safari or
     Firefox, open index.html in Chrome or Edge instead.
  3. Click "Help" at the top of the page for a short guide.

FINDING CASES
  - Age, year, sex and site (top left): type a minimum and/or maximum age
    or year, or leave a box empty for no limit. Ranges include both ends.
    Setting an age limit leaves out ECGs with unknown age. Untick a sex or
    site to leave it out.
  - Label filter (left side): for each diagnosis label, click "req" to require
    it or "excl" to exclude it. Choose whether a case needs ALL of the
    required labels or AT LEAST ONE of them. By default the labels are the
    physician overreads, and you can switch the label source at the top to
    the original MUSE statement or the ProtoECGNet fusion model's
    predictions.
  - Click "Random match" (or press R) to see a random ECG that matches the
    filter, or type an ECG ID and press Enter.
  - Use the Back / Forward buttons (or the left / right arrow keys) to go
    back through the cases you have viewed.
  - The right side shows the age, sex, year and site, and all 20 study
    labels with a dot under each label source that calls it positive. Rows
    where the sources disagree are highlighted. The model's yes/no comes
    from its probability and a per-label cutoff chosen on a separate
    validation set.

SELECTING CASES
  - Click "Select case" (or press S) to add the ECG to your list. You can
    then type a note, for example why you picked it. Click the button again
    to remove the case from your list.
  - Your list saves automatically in this browser on this computer, and is
    still there after you close and reopen the browser. It does NOT carry
    over to a different browser or computer.

SENDING YOUR SELECTIONS BACK
  - Click "Export CSV" (or "Export JSON"). The browser saves a file to your
    Downloads folder. Send that file back. It has no ECG tracings, but it
    does list the ECG and patient IDs with age, sex and year, so send it the
    way you would send other study data covered by the DUA.
  - Export every so often as a backup. To restore a list, or to move it to
    another browser, click "Import..." and choose an exported file.
